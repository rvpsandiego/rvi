Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Ramanuj Vidyapeeth International School

Admissions

Ramanuj Vidyapeeth International School (R V I) is a proposed public charter school serving San Diego County. It offers an inclusive, academically rigorous, and culturally responsive education through trilingual immersion in English, Hindi, and Sanskrit, combined with Project-Based Learning. The school is rooted in timeless values of Dharma and Sanskar.

Yes! Ramanuj Vidyapeeth is proposed as a tuition-free public charter school — open to all families in San Diego County. As a Countywide Benefit Charter School, there are no enrollment fees.

Note: Until the school is formally adopted by SDUSD, the program may be tuition-based. The long-term vision is a fully tuition-free public charter.

The proposed launch timeline is Fall 2027. In Year 1, the school will serve grades K–8, with plans to grow to a full K–12 program in subsequent years.

The school is designed to serve students from Kindergarten through Grade 12 (K–12). The school will begin with grades K–8 in Year 1 and expand to include grades 9–12 in subsequent years.

Academics

Our curriculum is fully aligned with California state standards and integrates STEM, Humanities, and Arts. Key features include:

  • Standards-aligned instruction with a global perspective
  • Project-Based Learning (PBL) as a core instructional model
  • Trilingual immersion in English, Hindi, and Sanskrit
  • Social-Emotional Learning (SEL)
  • Performance-based assessment to evaluate growth holistically

Project-Based Learning is a student-centered teaching method where students learn by actively engaging in real-world, meaningful projects. Instead of memorizing facts, students investigate complex questions, solve authentic problems, and create high-quality work products. At RVI, PBL builds 21st century skills including critical thinking, creativity, collaboration, and communication.

The Seal of Biliteracy is a California state award recognizing students who demonstrate proficiency in two or more languages. RVI's biliteracy pathway is designed to prepare students to earn this seal, which is recognized on high school diplomas and valued by colleges and employers. Students will develop proficiency in English alongside Hindi and/or Sanskrit.

RVI is committed to supporting every learner through a comprehensive support framework:

  • MTSS (Multi-Tiered System of Supports) — early identification and intervention
  • Special Education services for students with IEPs
  • English Learner support for students developing English proficiency
  • Inclusive classrooms that value neurodiversity
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL) — flexible instructional approaches

Programs

Sanskrit is one of the world's oldest and most systematic languages, connecting students to a rich tradition of ancient wisdom, philosophy, mathematics, and literature. Learning Sanskrit strengthens understanding of grammar and linguistics, enhances vocabulary across many world languages (including English), and connects students to their cultural heritage. It is also a key component of the school's values-based education rooted in Dharma and Sanskar.

No — students do not need to speak Hindi or Sanskrit at home. The school's immersive program is designed to teach these languages within the school environment. Families who speak these languages at home will find it enriching, but it is not a requirement for enrollment. All students begin their language journey at the school regardless of prior exposure.

RVI believes in celebrating the rich tapestry of cultural traditions. The school observes:

  • Rakhi (Raksha Bandhan)
  • Eid al-Fitr
  • Guru Nanak Jayanti
  • Diwali (Deepawali)
  • Holi (Festival of Colors)
  • Christmas

These celebrations are educational opportunities to discover the cultural significance and joyful traditions of key global festivals.

Students at RVI will have opportunities to engage in:

  • Inquiry-based projects and research
  • Student leadership programs
  • Community service initiatives
  • Cultural celebrations and performances
  • Technology integration and digital literacy

Full extracurricular program details will be announced closer to the school's launch in Fall 2027.
