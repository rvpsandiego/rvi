Guiding Principle

विद्या ददाति विनयम्, विनयाद् याति पात्रताम्।
पात्रत्‍वात् धनमाप्नोति, धनात् धर्मं ततः सुखम्।
Vidyā Dadāti Vinayam, Vinayād Yāti Pātratām |
Pātratvāt Dhanam Āpnoti, Dhanāt Dharmaṃ Tataḥ Sukham ||
विद्या ददाति विनयम् Knowledge imparts humility and discipline
विनयाद् याति पात्रताम् From humility comes worthiness and capability
पात्रत्‍वात् धनमाप्नोति Worthiness leads to the acquisition of wealth
धनात् धर्मं ततः सुखम् Wealth enables righteous actions (Dharma), and from Dharma comes true happiness

Mission

Ramanuj Vidyapeeth International School provides an inclusive, academically rigorous, and culturally responsive education through standards-aligned instruction, Project-Based Learning, and multilingual learning in English, Hindi, and Sanskrit. Rooted in timeless values, cultural traditions, and the principle of Dharma (universal ethical principle) — ethical responsibility, integrity, compassion, and service to others — Sanskar, we empower students to think critically, communicate confidently, respect diverse perspectives, and contribute meaningfully to society.

Vision

Our vision is to develop knowledgeable, compassionate, multilingual, and globally minded learners who are grounded in values and traditions while being prepared for the modern world. Guided by Dharma (universal ethical principle), students will develop integrity, self-discipline, respect, empathy, and a sense of responsibility toward their families, communities, humanity, and the environment.
